Most of what I'd want to say about a regulatory development doesn't fit in a
post on someone else's platform, and the version that does fit tends to lose
the part that mattered. So: a site of my own, where the length of a piece is
determined by the argument rather than by a character count.

I expect this to be occasional rather than regular, and closer to working
notes than to finished commentary. Some of it will be about the areas I
practise in. Some of it won't.

The usual caveat, which is genuinely meant: nothing here is legal advice, and
nothing here should be read as the position of my firm or its clients. It's
one lawyer thinking out loud.
